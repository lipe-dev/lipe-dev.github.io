# Artes para Botões

A repository of printable artwork for [[Futebol de Botão]] buttons. Fan-made
designs for anyone to print and assemble their own teams.

## What's In It

Every team ships in multiple formats:
- **AI** - Source files with alternate designs outside the artboard
- **SVG** - Vector format for any design tool (including free ones like Inkscape)
- **PDF** - Print-ready
- **PNG** - 300dpi raster for high-quality printing

Designs support standard vidrilha sizes: 45mm and 55mm. The vector formats
scale to whatever you need.

## The Teams

### Campeões Mundiais

Historical FIFA Club World Cup and Intercontinental Cup champions, ordered by
title count. Each uniform matches what the team wore when they won their most
iconic title:

| Team | Final | Opponent |
|------|-------|----------|
| Real Madrid | 2002 | Olimpia |
| Milan | 2007 | Boca Juniors |
| Corinthians | 2012 | Chelsea |
| Grêmio | 1983 | Hamburgo |
| Bayern | 2020 | Tigres |

### Série Não-Futebolistica

Sanrio character teams made for family members who needed their own buttons
but don't care about football clubs.

## The Process

Detailed assembly instructions live in the README. The short version: print on
photo paper, cut precisely, glue inside transparent acrylic buttons
(vidrilhas), paint the interior, sand the feet.

## Why Not Modern Teams?

Three reasons (maybe four, I can't count):
1. I don't follow modern football, I follow button football
2. Plenty of modern team artwork exists elsewhere
3. Modern football is sponsored by betting companies and I don't support that
4. The Corinthians of today only brings sadness
