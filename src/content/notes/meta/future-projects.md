# Future Projects

Things I want to build someday. Keeping them here so I don't forget.

## Lyst Revival

The [[Utyl Business Platforms]] supermarket price comparison idea, but with
modern tech and a different approach.

**Original concept:** Crowdsourced prices, optimized shopping lists by price
and location.

**Why it failed:** Tried to partner with supermarkets who didn't want price
transparency.

**New approach:** Skip the partnerships. Build it as a consumer tool, let
users contribute data, focus on community rather than B2B. PWA with barcode
scanning, Supabase backend, maybe some AI for receipt parsing.

The tech is a weekend project now. The question is whether there's a
sustainable community model without retailer buy-in.

## Digiploma-Web

Digital diplomas and certificates with QR code validation.

**The idea:** Issue credentials that can be printed but also verified online.
Scan the QR code, see if the diploma is legit. Useful for:
- Online course certificates
- Workshop attendance
- Any credential that gets faked

**Why build it:** Plenty of solutions exist, but building my own would be fun.
Could integrate with [[Supabase]] for auth and storage, generate PDFs with
embedded verification codes.

Not urgent. Just sounds like a satisfying weekend project.
